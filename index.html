What we're building next.

Two projects in active development: a calm way to learn Arabic, and a 3D maths game running on our own C++ and Vulkan engine.

LearnArabic

Learn to read Arabic properly, from the first stroke: positional forms, harakat and handwriting, with native audio on every item. It's calm, ad-free and fully offline, and it takes learners past A1 toward B2.

Mental maths, played like a game. It's a landscape 3D game on native C++ and Vulkan with Jolt physics, and its on-device ML reads handwritten answers and adapts to every player.

How we build

Small studio. No shortcuts.

Native, start to finish

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with baseline profiles and stability rules, so screens open fast and scroll smoothly on mid-range phones, not just flagships.

Private by default

Tafseer collects zero personal data. SnapDocx's AI runs on the phone with zero uploads. Habit Flow keeps your data on the device first and syncs only when you sign in.

Works offline

Saved Ayahs and recitations, every SnapDocx tool, every CheckMines board and listings you've already loaded in Kashmir Market all work with no connection.

Native code where it counts

SnapDocx uses a native C++ PDF engine, and Fast Maths runs on our own C++20 / Vulkan engine with Jolt physics and on-device ML.
